OUR STORY

Once upon a time, in a quiet little neighborhood, two children met as preschool classmates, their paths crossing like stars in the night sky. Life led them on separate journeys, and for years, they went their own ways, unaware that destiny had plans to bring them together again.

Fate reunited them in high school, and from the very first year, he noticed her charm and beauty. But it was on the first day of their third year that magic truly happened. As she walked into the classroom, time seemed to stand still, and in that moment, he knew his heart had found its home.

That girl, who enchanted him then, has now become his princess, his partner, and the love of his life. Together, they continue their own fairy tale, hand in hand, ready to write the many adventures that lie ahead.
